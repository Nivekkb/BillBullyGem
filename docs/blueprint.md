# **App Name**: BillBully

## Core Features:

- Bill Negotiation: Use AI to negotiate lower bills with service providers.
- Credit Report Integration: Connect to credit bureaus (Equifax and TransUnion in Canada) to pull credit reports.  User uploads of credit bureau responses will be parsed with AI.
- Dispute Letter Generation: Automatically generate personalized dispute letters for inaccurate credit report items.
- Automated Mailing: Automatically mail dispute letters to credit bureaus via API (Canada Post).
- Subscription Cancellation: Identify and cancel unwanted subscriptions using Plaid and AI to send cancellation requests.
- Dashboard: Display savings from bill negotiations, credit score improvements, and cancelled subscriptions in a visually appealing format.
- AI Compliance Tool: Ensure credit repair features remain compliant with Canadian consumer protection laws (federal Consumer Reporting Act; PIPEDA) .  Tool uses reasoning to assess legal compliance and adjusts operation.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Dark Blue (#24305E) to convey trust and reliability.
- Background color: Light blue (#E0F2F7), a very light tint of the primary hue, for a clean and modern look.
- Accent color: Teal (#33A19F) to highlight key actions and elements, analogous to dark blue in hue, yet providing a brightness/saturation contrast.
- Body font: 'Inter' sans-serif font. Suitable for the quantity of the body text expected in this app.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' sans-serif font. Pairs well with Inter and suits short headline texts.
- Use clean, modern icons to represent different bill categories, credit report items, and subscription services.
- Maintain a clean and organized layout with clear sections for each feature.
